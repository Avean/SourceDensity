\begin{abstract}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Problem: source density is often used in Hydra models, but its precise mathematical and biological role is not clear.
    \item Different Gierer--Meinhardt model variants have different properties, even when they are treated as versions of the same model.
    \item The goal of the paper is to separate what follows from the Gierer--Meinhardt mechanism itself from what genuinely requires source density.
    \item We show that source density is not necessary for basic regeneration or classical pattern formation.
    \item Source density becomes important when modelling positional memory and grafting/polarization experiments.
    \item Dynamic source density can generate long-lived transients or metastable peaks, but it does not necessarily produce stable stationary states at arbitrary positions.
    \item We propose an extension with a hysteretic head identity variable.
\end{itemize}
\end{abstract}
